# CONFLICT RESOLVER — MONOCLE

## Purpose
Resolve conflicts between overlapping capacities safely.

---

## Core Rule
Resolve by constraint, not preference.

Priority:
1) Reversibility
2) Exposure minimization
3) Auditability

---

## Resolution Order
1. Safety / irreversibility
2. Capacity constraints
3. Boundary integrity
4. Compliance & audit
5. Efficiency

---

## Tie-Breakers
- Downgrade capacity
- Defer action
- Seek written clarification
- Exit temporarily

---

## Invariants
- No action > wrong action
- Speed < safety
- Resolution must be explainable after the fact
